# Wildlife Overhaul

Purpose: More varied and more dangerous wildlife: new behaviours first, then new animals alongside the old ones.
Stage: unfinished

## Features

- [done] Cape buffalo charges the player instead of fleeing, gores on contact and charges again, one at a time - user: "good as a spike"
- [done] A gore knocks the player down; they get up unless it was the killing blow - not seen in game (Faint and Die seen from Lua)
- [in progress] Hyena, a new predator - step 1 of 4, the archetype, spawns and runs; hunting brain, sounds and placement, mesh to do #14
- [new] Fish shoals: the bird flock under water with a fish mesh - researched, nothing built
- [new] Ostrich: the chicken scaled up with an ostrich mesh - researched, nothing built
- [new] Lion: stalks and pounces, on the wildebeest rig - researched, nothing built
- [new] Crocodile, an ambusher at the waterline - in the design log's build order after the lion
- [new] Snake as a still hazard that bites and hisses - researched, nothing built
- [new] Pool plugin: placed animals sometimes become new ones - after the in-game check
- [scrapped] Buffalo head toss after a hit - user: useless, it played after stopping
