# Extending the World

**Status:** guidelines and an idea list, drawn from the [lore section](../lore/themes.md) on
2026-10-14. Nothing here is planned or built. Each idea carries a status; all live ones are `idea`
until someone picks one up.

## The goal

New content for Far Cry 2 should read as if it shipped: the same war, the same people, the same
bleakness. This page sets the rules for staying in tone, then lists what could be added, rated for
how well it fits the lore and how hard it is to mod.

Every rule below rests on a lore page, and those pages cite the game's own strings. Where a rule goes
beyond what the strings say, it is marked as this page's reading.

## What the game insists on

Five things come back in every faction, mission and character. New content should carry at least one
of them and contradict none.

### No heroes

Everyone with a gun is in it for something. The factions state no program beyond nationalism, order
and plunder, and the buddies say nobody remembers why they fight
([factions](../lore/factions.md#what-each-faction-says-it-stands-for)). The mercenaries are assumed to
work for money, and they do, the buddies included: "I'm sorry, mate. Just business."
([mercenaries and money](../lore/themes.md#mercenaries-and-money)). Every patron betrays the player,
and every betrayal he suffers is one he can also commit
([betrayal and mercy](../lore/themes.md#betrayal-and-mercy)).

**So:** a new character may have a decent motive, but not clean hands. Flora "chooses her allegiances
based on ideology rather than cash" and still steals NGO medicine to sell or hand out
([characters](../lore/characters.md#the-buddies)). That is the ceiling for virtue.

### The cycle of violence

Every cease-fire breaks, usually by the player's hand, and every peace is a pause the strong use to
regroup ([cease-fires and peace](../lore/themes.md#cease-fires-and-peace)). When the factions finally
unite, their first act together is to hunt the refugees, and the interim government they form fails:
"Anarchy rules to this day." ([how the factions end](../lore/factions.md#how-the-factions-end)).

**So:** nothing a mod adds should end the war or fix anything lasting. A job may win a battle; it
should cost someone else, and start the next one.

### Everyone profits from the war

Diamonds are the only money, and the one mine that should produce them is a lie told to keep soldiers
fighting ([diamonds](../lore/themes.md#diamonds)). The Jackal arms both sides; the player's own vendor
wrecks rivals' convoys; aid is captured and resold; foreign companies pump the water, fuel and gas
([arms trade](../lore/themes.md#the-arms-trade), [outsiders and aid](../lore/themes.md#outsiders-aid-and-africa)).
The lieutenants fear peace because it ends their jobs.

**So:** a new group needs a business, and the business needs the war. Ask who pays, in what, and who
loses if the shooting stops.

### The player is complicit

He is sent to kill an arms dealer and buys every gun from one. He is the instrument that breaks every
cease-fire. The Underground speaks coldly to him once he is feared, and finally stops dealing with
him ([malaria and medicine](../lore/themes.md#malaria-and-medicine)). The Jackal ends by saying the
player is infected too ([disease as the war](../lore/themes.md#disease-as-the-war)).

**So:** new jobs should be things a mercenary would be paid to do, and should not flatter him. A
choice between two bad outcomes fits; a moral reward for doing right does not.

### Decay and sickness

The player has malaria from the start and never loses it. Medicine is a weapon and a currency. The
Jackal's image for the war is a disease that "the longer you stay, the deeper the virus goes". The
country has emptied: 55 placed civilians against about a thousand soldiers, nearly half of them hidden
by the Underground ([catalogue](../lore/set-dressing/catalogue.md#civilians)). The soldiers are bored
men who smoke, piss and play dice more than they stand guard
([what the soldiers do](../lore/set-dressing/catalogue.md#what-the-soldiers-do)).

**So:** add ruin, not bustle. A new place should look used up, and a new crowd is almost always wrong.

A sixth theme sits under these: **the truth doesn't get out**. Reuben's tapes are seized and his
coverage ignored, and the Jackal's identity "remains a mystery"
([information](../lore/themes.md#information)). New content should not explain the Jackal, name the
player's employer, or let the outside world find out.

## What the world never does

| Never | Why, from the lore | This means for a mod |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A clear good side** | The nearest is the Underground, a small civilian network that trades pills for errands and shuts out the infamous ([factions](../lore/factions.md#other-organised-groups)) | no liberators, no righteous rebels, no rescue that costs nothing |
| **Fantasy or advanced technology** | The guns are Cold War stock and dug-up relics; the air war is a Hind wreck and a chopper heard off-stage; communication is cell towers, phones and one radio station ([arms trade](../lore/themes.md#the-arms-trade)) | no drones, satellites, power armour, prototype weapons that work better than the AK, or anything a 2008 African militia could not buy second-hand |
| **Anything outside its time and place** | Late 2008, from the Jackal tapes' recording dates; an unnamed, inland African country with paired-name regions ([timeline](../lore/timeline.md#dates-and-durations), [places](../lore/places.md#the-country)) | keep the country unnamed; foreigners come from real places (Angola, Belgium, Ukraine, Moldova, Jordan, the PMCs), the setting does not; no real-world named conflict inside the country |
| **A resolution** | Every ending fails; the coda says anarchy rules | no "the war is over", no rebuilt town |
| **Crowds** | The country has emptied (see above) | a market of fifty people breaks the setting more than any texture could |
| **An answer to its mysteries** | The Jackal's name, the employer, what GRIN stands for, the predecessor's fate: all left open ([characters](../lore/characters.md#the-predecessor)) | do not answer them; new content can deepen them |
| **Malaria cured** | "Malaria never really goes away." | pills manage it; nothing ends it |

## How to write in the game's voice

These are this page's reading of the strings, not rules the game states.

- **Flat delivery, dark jokes.** The mercenaries joke ("It didn't mean short-bus slow"); the warlords
  shout slogans; the Jackal explains atrocity like a salesman ([tone](../lore/jackal-tapes.md#tone)).
  Nobody makes speeches about suffering except the people causing it.
- **Every name is local and plausible.** The country has paired regional names (Leboa-Sako,
  Bowa-Seko, Pelo-Laboso), a Setswana-sounding royal title (Kgosi), and foreign companies with bland
  corporate names (Occidental Growers, Mertens-Segolo, TaeMoCo). Keep to that.
- **A job briefing names a place on the map, a boss, a target and a payment in stones.**
- **The world data trap:** red soldiers are the UFLL, blue are the APR, and nearly every placed guard
  is blue whatever he guards ([reading the world](../lore/set-dressing/reading-the-world.md#red-is-the-ufll-blue-is-the-apr)).
  A new post's allegiance should be read from its posters, and set with the instance's army field
  ([adding an NPC](../modding/adding-an-npc.md#sides-and-hostility)).

## The idea list

**Lore fit:** ★★★ anchored by a string or a placed scene; ★★ consistent, with no direct anchor;
★ a stretch; ✗ against the tone.

**Feasibility**, from the modding pages:

- **data:** placing entities, layers and strings, all of which have proven or built tooling
- **data + script:** also needs a Domino graph or a `missiontools.lua` handler
- **plugin:** needs an FCSE plugin
- **blocked:** a named blocker stands in the way

**Status:** `idea` (open), `parked` (fits, but deliberately set aside), `rejected` (against the tone).

### Rejected outright

Against the tone, whatever the feasibility:

- zombies, mutants, any supernatural element
- drones, satellite targeting, power armour, futuristic weapons
- naming the country, or placing it on a real map
- revealing the Jackal's real name or the player's employer
- a cure for malaria, or a rebuilt town
- crowds of happy civilians, tourism, a functioning economy

## Open questions

- **Child soldiers.** The tapes mention them and the game never shows one. Whether a mod should is a
  matter of taste and of the maintainer's call, not lore. Parked, not rejected.
- **Faction skirmishes.** The maintainer reports they were designed and switched off. No string or
  document in the repository confirms it; it is recorded here as maintainer-reported. Whether
  re-enabling them fits depends on why they were cut: "not enough extra feeling" is a design verdict,
  not a lore one.
- **How far to push "no heroes".** The Underground and Doctor Obua are the game's nearest thing to
  decent people. New characters in that mould are allowed by this page; how many before the balance
  tips is not.
- **Pelo-Laboso's look.** The lore gives only "rough terrain… no vehicles can pass". Everything else
  about it would be invention.
