# What players ask for

Wishes for Far Cry 2 mods and remakes, as players state them, by subject. Each wish names its source
and who raised it; the score is the upvotes of the comment it came from, so wishes from one comment
share a number. What the game holds of the cut features players remember is in
[Cut and dormant content](../cut-and-dormant/overview.md).

:::note[Community-reported]
These are requests, quoted from their sources. Nothing here is checked against the game.
:::

On landmark navigation, OP replies that it should be an optional difficulty setting.

Campaign co-op never existed in the shipped game: OP meant online multiplayer, which a community site
keeps running. FarCryRedux says old tech pictures of a co-op project exist, but nothing is in
development.

## Mods players point to

- **Scubrah's Patch:** brings back the compass and using the watch in free roam, by OP's account
  (OP has not tried it). OP also says "the mod" fixes enemy respawning, presumably this patch.
- **Realism+ Redux:** removes the player marker from the map.
- **ReShade:** used to remove the brown look.
