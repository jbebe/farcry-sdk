# What players ask for

Wishes for Far Cry 2 mods and remakes, as players state them, by subject. Each wish names who raised
it and its source. A score is the upvotes of the comment it came from, so wishes from one comment share
a number; only source A carries scores. What the game holds of the cut features players remember is in
[Cut and dormant content](../cut-and-dormant/overview.md).

:::note[Community-reported]
These are requests and player explanations, quoted from their sources. Nothing here is checked
against the game.
:::

Players' explanation of the fast respawns (B, hedged "IIRC"): the world is split into chunks, and
leaving a chunk resets it, respawning its enemies, vehicles and consumables (Mister-Lime); BeezNest96
puts it down to the memory limits of an early open-world engine.

## Survival, disease and medicine

All from B.

- **Eating, drinking and sleeping** (OP): heat exhaustion and dehydration in deserts, so you drive or
  travel at night; beds in towns and safe houses; food from vendors, scavenging, hunting and foraging.
- **Diseases that happen in play, not by script** (OP), each with its own penalties:

  | Disease | Cause |
  | --- | --- |
  | Malaria | staying in the jungle without repellent |
  | Parasites | eating uncooked meat |
  | Tetanus | cuts from rusty melee weapons |
  | Rabies | animal bites |
  | Infection | untreated wounds |

- **Medicine** bought or scavenged; UN faction missions unlock permanent vaccines (OP).
- Against: keep malaria the only disease, because it is tied to the story (Altruistic_Truck2421);
  malaria can feel tiresome (brokedownsystem).

## Progression

All from B.

- **Skills that improve with use**, not from XP or skill trees (OP): scope sway, reload speed, bandage
  strength, skinning yield; good nutrition speeds learning; named levels from "amateur" to "phenom"
  instead of numbers.
- **No XP or skill trees at all**, because they reward some playstyles over others; diamonds and buddy
  missions stay the progression (Mister-Lime, cocukyokedici16).
- **Permadeath balanced by several mercenaries** you switch between (BeezNest96): enemies die quickly,
  buddies can rescue you, serious injuries take long to heal, and you die for good only after a real
  mistake.
- **A permadeath mode** (EnjayDutoit).

## Enemies and wildlife

All from B.

- **More enemy types** (EnjayDutoit): militia with pistols and machetes; riflemen; heavies in vests and
  helmets; breachers with shotguns, SMGs and riot shields; flamethrower and molotov troops; grenadiers;
  officers who raise morale; radiomen who call reinforcements.
- **Predators:** lions, leopards and crocodiles (OP); also elephants, hippos, rhinos, wild dogs and
  hyenas (EnjayDutoit).
- Dangerous wildlife kept in check, not constant attacks as in Far Cry 5 (Mister-Lime).
- Animals give no loot; instead a trophy-hunter quest line ends in a lion or elephant hunt (OP).
- A pet hyena (Aijin28).

## Economy and side content

All from B.

- **A black market** for guns, contraband, ivory, pelts and diamonds (OP). Against: diamonds stay the
  only economy (Mister-Lime).
- **A UN refugee camp** (OP): you escort refugees there, donate resources to upgrade its hospital, soup
  kitchen, market and lodging, and its people give side missions.
- **Side missions from unique characters** (OP): a British wildlife photographer with a shot list ("lion
  eating a gazelle"); an arms dealer who wants specific models or hijacked shipments; a bounty hunter
  with assassination contracts.
- More side content in the style of the Jackal tapes, without goofy characters (Mister-Lime).

## Mods players point to

- **Scubrah's Patch:** brings back the compass and using the watch in free roam, by A's OP's account
  (OP has not tried it); OP also says "the mod" fixes enemy respawning, presumably this patch. In B,
  NiuMeee credits it with time-based respawns.
- **Realism+ Redux:** removes the player marker from the map (A); safe houses appear only once found
  (Mister-Lime, B).
- **ReShade:** used to remove the brown look (A).
