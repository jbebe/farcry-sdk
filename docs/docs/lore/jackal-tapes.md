# The Jackal tapes

Seventeen recordings of Reuben Oluwagembi's interviews with the Jackal. The game ships their titles,
dates and one-line descriptions as text (`JackalTapes/A1JT00`–`A2JT16_DESC`), but not the words: the
recordings are unsubtitled. This page adds what the recordings say, taken from a fan transcription on
the [Far Cry wiki](https://farcry.fandom.com/wiki/Jackal_Tapes).

:::note[Community-reported]
The transcripts are the wiki's, not read from the game's files, and were not checked against the
audio. The wiki's titles and recording dates match the game's own tape strings one for one, so each
transcript is at least filed under the right tape. Quotes from them are cited as (wiki, *title*).
:::

## What each tape says

Reuben hands the player a tape at Mike's, which the wiki says is tape 00
(`a1gm00_grindelivery.a1gm00_mission`, `JackalTapes.New`). Each tape picked up after that adds one to a
count (`sidemissions.carvertapes_missions:f_69_Out`). By the wiki's account the engine plays the next
tape in number order, wherever it was found. Reuben asks, the
Jackal answers; the Bowa-Seko tapes are mostly the Jackal alone.

## What the tapes add

**Community-reported (the transcripts state it)**

- **Reuben is the interviewer.** The Jackal calls him by name and says Reuben "sold me on doing these
  dumb interviews" (wiki, *The Humanist*). The game's own descriptions are written in his voice: "One of
  my final recordings with the Jackal." (`JackalTapes/A1JT01_DESC`).
- **Reuben is African and the war is in his home.** "Why here? Why my home?" (wiki, *Home*). He says the
  same in the game: "I'm African. This is my home." (`5107230`, Reuben).
- **The Jackal's past.** He served in a navy that shipped covert weapons, and spent a year in Black Beach
  prison (wiki, *Career Counseling*, *Rat-Catcher*).
- **Where the guns come from.** Ceasefires elsewhere, Liberia named, and old European colonial
  stocks; the Soviet surplus of 1989–91 is "all over" (wiki, *Circulation*).
- **His business with both sides.** He armed the APR and the UFLL and calls their détente good for
  trade (wiki, *Stability*). He sold Mbantuwe C4 six months before the recording (wiki, *Smuggling*).
- **His name.** The jackal that steals the lion's share (wiki, *Lion's Share*).
- **The interview ended violently.** The last-dated tape has the Jackal finding Reuben's truck
  destroyed and its recorder gone, not knowing whether Reuben is alive (wiki, *Unrecognizable*).

**Implied**

- **The tapes set out his arc.** In September and early October he takes no side and sells for
  profit (*Stability*, *Economics*). By the October tapes he speaks of boy soldiers, a dying man and
  "what if you save a thousand" (*Stealing Boots*, *Gutshot*, *Numbers*). In the game he moves the same
  way: in Act 2 he "GAVE" the guns away (`4560002`, Greaves), and in the end he kills the leaders to get
  the civilians out. *Numbers* is the reasoning of the finale's choice.
- **Tape 00 names a game system.** *Infamy* describes the terror a mercenary spreads, and infamy is what
  the game tracks: the Underground refuses medicine to a player "too infamous for your medicine"
  (`sidemissions.grin_missions:f_184_A_is_True`).
- **The monster warning is aimed at the player.** "If you succumb to the horror, then you become the
  monster" (wiki, *Infamy*) and the rat-catcher's "Realization of what he'd turned into" (wiki,
  *Rat-Catcher*) prepare the Jackal's line in Act 2: "Wake up. I used to BE you." (`4577731`, Jackal).
- **Gutshot is the buddy's mercy kill.** The begging, dying man is what a downed buddy becomes when the
  player chooses to finish him (see [How the campaign runs](./missions/overview.md#how-a-buddy-fares-in-his-fight)).
- **How the tapes were scattered.** Reuben's truck was destroyed and his recorder taken (wiki,
  *Unrecognizable*), and when the player meets him, men keep coming for his tapes: "You people never
  stop. Here's the bloody tape." (`4577380`, Reuben). The tapes in the field are the stolen ones.
- **Reuben's "dead" interviewee is still unexplained.** At the twelfth tape Reuben says "This man I
  interviewed here, he died a few months ago. These are his famous last words, I suppose." (`4911521`,
  Reuben). The tapes show the Jackal unsure whether Reuben died, not the reverse.

## Tone

**Implied**, from the transcripts:

- Calm, conversational and contemptuous: a salesman explaining his trade, then a man who has stopped
  pretending it is a trade.
- He attacks the listener's comfort more than the warlords: Western taxpayers, the Pentagon, the media,
  Reuben's idea that he could never sell arms (*Gluten-Free*, *Necessary Evil*, *The Humanist*).
- Bleak anecdotes told flatly: the rat-catcher, the boy taking boots, the gut-shot man. The Jackal
  calls those men idiots, but the stories stay with him.
- He sounds like the game's Jackal: the disease metaphors and "every cell is infected" of his scenes
  (`4577606`, Jackal) grow out of the "no sense in it at all" of *No Sense*.

## The wiki against the scripts

- **When Reuben appears.** The wiki says Reuben comes to the bar once at least one tape has been found.
  The scripts call him only at every fourth tape, at Mike's in Leboa-Sako and the Marina in Bowa-Seko
  (`sidemissions.carvertapes_missions:f_34_*`).
- **The order.** The wiki's "same order regardless of where players pick them up" agrees with the
  script, which only counts tapes and leaves the choice of recording to the engine
  (`JackalTapes.New`, `NewJackalTapeFound`).
- **A playback bug.** The wiki reports that since the final patch every Bowa-Seko tape after Stealing
  Boots plays the same recording. Not checked here. The tapes' audio could not be found in the game's
  archives (see [Sources and citations](./sources.md#open-questions)).
- **Small text differences.** The wiki copies the descriptions with slips, such as "fresh contacts" for
  the game's "fresh conflicts" (`JackalTapes/A1JT02_DESC`). The game's text is used here.
