The analysed Limited Navigation choice, an option the Final release adds: no player position or
undiscovered outposts on the map, only the player on the GPS, no objective arrows, and road signs
that no longer light up toward missions. The save-point icons are gone in every variant, through the
engine. The Full Navigation variant keeps the icon models and the sign tints; the pages say exactly
what it keeps.
