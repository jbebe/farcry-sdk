---
title: Limited saving
kind: bundle
claims:
  - "Optional feature that disables quicksave and saving from the pause menu - limiting saving to safehouses, gun shops, bus stops and mission givers"
includes: [saving-no-pause-save, saving-quicksave-unbound]
---

The analysed Limited Saving choice: the quicksave and quickload keys are unbound in the controls
files, and the engine's pause-menu save is disabled. The Save Anywhere variant differs from it in
exactly those two places, its patch archive's controls files and its `Dunia.dll`.
