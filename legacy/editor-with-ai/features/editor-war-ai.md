Princeton73's own feature, the one the mod is named for. The editor's game mode gets the campaign's
AI services, the palette gets the campaign's characters, and the faction leaders get a fighting
loadout.

The weapon packs, AI odds and materials the added services read are shared with the campaign. Those
edits are on the `gameplay` and `materials` pages. [`materials-transparency`](materials-transparency.md)
in particular lets every AI, in the editor too, see through walls.
